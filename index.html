I am a Director of Software Engineering and the Autonomous Agents ML Research Lead at Salesforce in London.

Previously, I was a Founding Member of Technical Staff (Research Scientist) at Convergence, before we were acquired by Salesforce. Before that, I worked as a Research Scientist at Spotify, and did my PhD at the University of Cambridge in collaboration with Microsoft Research.

🎓 Google Scholar - 📄 CV - 💻 GitHub - 💼 LinkedIn - 📧 email

I am interested in developing safe and effective machine learning systems that can successfully complete complex tasks in the real world. My work has often explored (inverse) reinforcement learning, imitation learning and simulation in order to learn from intrinsic rewards and humans, applied most recently to large language/multimodal models for autonomous computer-using agents.
